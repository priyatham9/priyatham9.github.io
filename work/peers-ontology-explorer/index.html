Chemical Peers Ontology Explorer

A portable, Workshop-style app over a Foundry-style ontology of US chemical plants. It joins three public sources (EPA Risk Management Program filings and accident histories, OSHA Form 300A injury logs, OSHA Severe Injury Reports) into 12 object types and 15 link types. It runs entirely in your browser from one vendored JSON file. It needs no Palantir account and uses no employer data. It is an independent design and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Palantir.

    5. Source and limits

    • Data: data.json in this folder, generated from the committed files of at commit (paths: ). The page does not fetch anything else.
    • Size cap: the full export is about 21 MB, so only are embedded. are counts only. Plants are therefore browsable only through their EPA registration; OSHA-only plants are not listed.
    • Holdings are the ten largest per plant (the source's own export limit). The match confidence is the ontology's own rubric over the join method and has not been validated against a third source.
    • The join between EPA and OSHA records is by address and name, not by a shared identifier. Treat each match as a hypothesis the actions above let an analyst review.
