        Contract format, guarantees & limits
        
      
          
      
            
      What the contract declares
      
            
      
              units declare dimension vectors and positive scales
              to canonical units. ratioConcepts declare numerator
              and denominator semantic roles independently of input bindings.
              Rates must satisfy both the dimension quotient and the conversion
              equation: calculation multiplier × output-unit scale =
              numerator-unit scale ÷ denominator-unit scale.
            
      
            
      
              types declare fields, links and
              uniqueBy reporting keys. Every reporting-key
              component must be present, even when its general-purpose link is
              optional. Every numeric binding declares its expected
              unit, concept, grain and
              aggregation. Rate signatures also carry
              rateScale, the inverse canonical output-unit scale.
              This output basis can differ from the numerical calculation
              multiplier after an explicit unit conversion.
            
      
            
      
              objects provide field values and reference targets.
              metrics use sum or rate;
              actions compare a typed metric with a declared
              threshold. A sum’s preserveKeys require shared
              context, such as one reporting year.
            
      
            
      
              Start from the visible JSON schema and replace its contents. Each
              of types, objects, metrics and actions accepts at most 100
              entries. Fields accept finite numbers or strings. The evaluator
              supports no arbitrary code.
            
      
          
      
          
      
            
      What is checked
      
            
      
              References, declared value constraints, reporting-key uniqueness,
              binding signatures, ratio unit algebra and semantic roles,
              complete reporting identities, matching numerator/denominator
              cohorts, shared aggregation context, positive denominators,
              nonadditive measures, duplicate lineage and dependency cycles.
            
      
            
      
              Reachability is exact for the declared graph. An undeclared
              dependency cannot be discovered. Labels, contracts, criticality
              and thresholds are supplied claims; the tool does not verify their
              truth or certify the source data.
            
      
            
      
              The constructed EHS fixture illustrates denominator and
              case-definition semantics. Its review thresholds are not validated
              safety limits, and the rates do not establish risk, causality or
              cross-site comparability.
