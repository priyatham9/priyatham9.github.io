Scores are supplied values, not verified probabilities. Average precision describes ranking; Brier error compares each score with its binary label. Neither verifies feature availability or generalization to new data.

Retrospective ranking check

Ranks use the complete accepted cohort. Exact ties break by original row order. Changing this share leaves average precision, Brier scores and ranks unchanged.
