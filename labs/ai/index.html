A TEST OF DISCRIMINATION

Evidence, before confidence.

This benchmark probes co-hyponym substitution: an answer about a neighboring entity instead of the entity asked about. The wrong-answer examples are authored probes, not observed model failures.

Inspect the reference and its clause, compare both members of a pair, then use the rubric to design an evaluation. No live model, generated answer, or model accuracy is represented.
