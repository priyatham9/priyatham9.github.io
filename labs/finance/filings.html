03 / WHAT THE REPORTS EXPLAIN—AND LEAVE OPEN

Scope evidence is not a reconciliation.

The residual remains unreconciled in this audit. FX, acquisitions, disposals, noncash changes and classification may affect the relationship. Their individual contributions are not estimated here. These two selected company cases do not estimate how frequently the issue occurs.

04 / REPRODUCE THE REVIEW

Pinned facts. Explicit selection.

Each fiscal year uses its own original annual filing, including the opening balance reported there. Selection requires exact accession, filing date, USD units and period. Identical duplicates collapse; conflicting duplicates fail. Fiscal-year labels and calendar frames do not select values. Later comparatives and amendments cannot silently overwrite the original.

Comparing the common inventory/payables subset aligns the arithmetic scope. It does not make different businesses or fiscal calendars economically comparable. The source reports disclose whole millions; small differences must be read at that precision.
