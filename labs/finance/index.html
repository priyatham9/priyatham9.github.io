Model method & boundaries 01
AR = annual revenue ÷ 365 × DSO
Inventory = annual COGS ÷ 365 × DIO
AP = annual COGS ÷ 365 × DPO
Net working capital = AR + inventory − AP
Cash cycle = DSO + DIO − DPO
Cash released = baseline NWC − scenario NWC

Constant annual run rate; purchases approximate COGS. Excludes seasonality, growth, taxes, FX, bad debts, prepayments and minimum order effects. Cash release is a one-time steady-state balance change, not recurring savings or profit. Display values are rounded; calculations retain full precision.
