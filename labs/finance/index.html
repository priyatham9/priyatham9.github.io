Where the cash sits

Receivables + inventory − payables = net working capital

A lower number has a cost.

COLLECTION / DSOCollect sooner

May require tighter terms or discounts. Check customer retention and discount cost.

INVENTORY / DIOHold less inventory

May increase stockouts or expedite costs. Check service levels and demand variability.

PAYMENT / DPOPay suppliers later

May reduce discounts or stress suppliers. Check contract terms and supply continuity.

The model measures cash tied up, not profit or risk. A cash release does not automatically create economic value.

Exact formulas, assumptions & boundaries
AR = annual revenue ÷ 365 × DSO
Inventory = annual COGS ÷ 365 × DIO
AP = annual COGS ÷ 365 × DPO
Net working capital = AR + inventory − AP
Cash conversion cycle = DSO + DIO − DPO
Cash released = baseline NWC − scenario NWC

Uses a constant annual run rate with no seasonality, growth, taxes, FX, bad debts, prepayments or minimum order effects. DSO uses revenue; DIO and DPO use cost of goods sold. Payables assumes purchases approximate COGS. A lower NWC balance represents a one-time steady-state cash release, not recurring annual savings. Display values are rounded; calculations retain full precision.
