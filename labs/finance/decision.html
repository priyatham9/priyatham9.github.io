What this model assumes

Receivables = net revenue × DSO ÷ 365. Inventory and payables use variable COGS × their days ÷ 365; purchases approximate COGS. Customer discounts reduce revenue; supplier premiums increase COGS. The release is current working capital minus policy working capital. Sales declines can release cash without improving operations.

NPV = release − setup − annual commercial cost × annuity factor − terminal reinvestment ÷ (1 + rate)years. Equivalently, annual value = rate × release − annual cost − annualized setup. Taxes, seasonality, growth, fixed costs and changes in financing access are excluded. Establish achievable operating days and customer/supplier responses before adopting a policy.
