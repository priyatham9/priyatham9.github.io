Methodology & data definitions

Each lead is counted once per milestone reached. Inbound and outbound are mutually exclusive and sum to the all-source cohort at every milestone. Historical wins are observed through 30 Apr. Not won = entered − won, including unresolved leads at cutoff. Not advanced = previous milestone − next milestone; this is not a formal closed-lost classification. Source comparisons are descriptive; different lead quality and selection effects may explain differences.

All proposals have the same assumed opportunity to close in the scenario. No timing, deal value, intervention cost or causal uplift is modeled. Source attribution and milestone definitions must be validated before applying this method to real CRM data.

Stage conversion = next milestone ÷ preceding milestone
Expected wins = proposals reached × assumed close rate
Incremental wins = expected wins − observed wins
Cohort win rate = observed wins ÷ entered leads
About this dataset & workspace

All counts and scenarios are synthetic. This independent demonstration contains no employer data, live CRM integration or performance claim. The aggregate dataset has no individual lead records. Inputs are reflected in the URL; share a scenario to preserve them, or reset to restore the baseline. CSV exports include the selected cohort, assumptions and formulas.
