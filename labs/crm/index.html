Model, definitions & data quality

Each lead is counted once per milestone reached. Sources are mutually exclusive and sum to the all-source cohort at every stage. Historical won deals are final as of the observation cutoff. Lost / not won = entered − won, including unresolved leads at cutoff; this is not a formal closed-lost rate. All synthetic proposals have the same assumed opportunity to close in the scenario. No timing, value, intervention cost or causal uplift is modeled. Attribution and stage definitions must be validated before applying this method to real CRM data.

Expected wins = proposals reached × selected closing rate
Incremental wins = expected wins − observed wins
Cohort win rate = observed wins ÷ leads entered
