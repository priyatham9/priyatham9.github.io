03 / What would resolve the ranking?

Evidence before spend

These are best-case counts of favorable resolutions, not promises about the outcomes. Verify observation dates and final dispositions before treating an unresolved case as a nonwin.

A channel budget decision also needs acquisition cost, customer contribution, capacity, and evidence about the incremental effect of changing allocation. Conversion rankings alone cannot answer it.

Audit trail

For each segment: lower = wins ÷ total; upper = (wins + unresolved) ÷ total. Common-mix bounds use your target weights. The source A − B difference runs from A lower − B upper to A upper − B lower. No independent-censoring assumption is imposed.
