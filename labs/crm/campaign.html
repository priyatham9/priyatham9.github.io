RETROSPECTIVE HELD-OUT RANKING

The highest-scored 10%

Both models rank the complete later batch together using scores, not outcomes. This assumes the batch is available; it is not an online schedule or a prospectively chosen contact policy.

Prediction is not incremental impact. Precision among highly ranked records is an observed subscription rate. It does not estimate how many subscriptions a new call would cause or the value of a different contact policy.

Exact metrics, counts and uncertainty
Do the scores behave like probabilities?

These are uncalibrated logistic-model outputs. A score of 0.8 is not a verified 80% chance for an individual. Fixed score bins compare average predictions with observed outcome rates in the later holdout.
